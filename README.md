# ClipIT

ClipIT is a Windows app that finds video clips across the internet, keeps the ones that fit your rules, and downloads them one at a time.

## Download

**[Download ClipIT-Setup.exe](https://github.com/softa-arch/ClipIT/releases/latest/download/ClipIT-Setup.exe)** and open it. That one file is all you need.

1. Choose the language and the folder. It proposes `Program Files\ClipIT`.
2. Press Next. "Create a desktop icon" is ticked; press Install.
3. Windows asks once for permission to create the folder. Choose Yes.

The first time ClipIT opens, it asks for an **access code**. You get one from the person who gave you ClipIT. You type it once; the PC remembers it until ClipIT is uninstalled.

Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown ("Windows protected your PC" → More info → Run anyway): the app is not code-signed.

Already have ClipIT? When a new version is out, a **New update** button appears at the top of the window. One click updates it.

## What it does

- You describe the clips you are after in your own words, for example "GTA 6 clips where a bug or something funny happens, or a streamer reacts".
- It searches YouTube, Twitch, Kick, Reddit, X, TikTok and other sites.
- **Skills** go further than a search. The first one, *GTA 6 Clips*, searches every site clips are uploaded to, then Claude looks at the videos themselves and keeps the ones in which something funny, a fail, a bug or a crash really happens, with the moment and the name of the streamer or uploader.
- On YouTube it also finds clips whose title does not name the game: uploads straight from a PlayStation, and clips titled after the game's characters or places.
- Rules decide what stays: at least 720p, landscape, length, views, "pure clip" (no edits, no compilations), and rules of your own.
- With the downloader installed it looks inside each clip: real picture size and length, and how many hard cuts it has.
- With Claude connected, it reads each clip's title, description and viewer comments to tell whether the clip is what you described, and says why.
- Click a clip to play it in the app. Right-click to copy the link, open it on the web, or download just that clip.
- Saved clips download one after another into a folder you choose.

## Good to know

- **Claude** is optional. ClipIT uses the Claude Code program that you install and sign in to yourself; it never asks for your Claude password. Searching X, TikTok and other sites, and reading comments, need it.
- **Twitch** works best with its free API key, added under Settings → Connections. **Reddit** can be searched directly once Reddit has approved your request for API access; until then Claude searches it on the web.
- The app is offered in English, Slovak, Czech, German, Spanish and French, and Claude can translate it into any other language.
- Download only clips you have the right to use.
